Thaana PDF to Word

Turn Dhivehi PDFs, scans and photos into clean text and Word files, with no scrambled letters or misplaced fili. Made for teachers.

Download (Windows & Mac) User manual (PDF)

What it does

  • Opens typed Dhivehi PDFs and puts every letter and fili in the right order
  • Copy and paste into Word, or save a ready Word file (right-to-left, justified)
  • Tables stay tables, numbered lists stay lists
  • Reads scans and photos of printed pages (PNG, JPEG, TIFF and more), and newspaper pages column by column
  • Works on Windows and Mac. Everything stays on your computer, nothing is uploaded, and no internet is needed

New in version 0.4

  • Much smaller: the Windows download is about 10 MB instead of about 120 MB
  • Faster: PDFs open almost instantly, and scans are read about twice as fast
  • Reads scans far better: on a clean printed page, about 94% of the letters and half of the words come out exactly right (it was about 60% and 12%)
  • Tables: a sentence that wraps inside a table cell now stays in that cell
  • Fewer lost letters: the last fili of words, Dhivehi commas (،) and Arabic words inside Dhivehi sentences are no longer dropped

How to get it

  1. Click Download and open the newest release.
  2. Windows: download the setup.exe file and run it. It is small (about 10 MB).
  3. Windows, without installing: download the portable.zip file, unzip it anywhere (even a USB stick) and double-click Thaana PDF to Word.exe. Good for school computers where you can't install programs.
  4. Mac: choose the .dmg marked aarch64 for Apple-chip Macs (M1 and later) or x64 for Intel Macs. Open it and drag the app into Applications.

The first time you open it

The app is free and not "signed", so your computer shows a warning once. It is not a virus.

  • Windows: "Windows protected your PC" - click More info, then Run anyway.
  • Mac: open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

Beta - what to expect

Typed PDFs work very well: their text comes out exactly. Scans and photos are read one page in about a second, but most pages still have some wrong or missing fili, so always check the result before you use it. Handwriting is not supported yet.

Feedback
